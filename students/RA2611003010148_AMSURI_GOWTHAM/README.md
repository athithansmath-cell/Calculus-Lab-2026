## Period 2 (50 Mins): Multivariable Limits, Partials, Euler’s Theorem & Jacobians
1. **Limit, Continuity & Geometrical Meaning:** Examine $f(x, y) = \frac{20xy}{x^2 + y^2}$ for $(x,y) \neq (0,0)$ and $f(0,0) = 0$. Test continuity along $y = mx$ and $y = x^2$, compute $f_x(0,0)$ and $f_y(0,0)$ from first principles, and state their geometrical interpretation.
2. **Total Derivative & Implicit Function:**
   * Find $\frac{dz}{dt}$ at $t = 0$ for $z = 4x^2 y + 5xy^2$, where $x = e^{4t}$ and $y = \sin(5t)$.
   * Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ and $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$ for the implicit curve $x^3 + y^3 + 60xy - 189 = 0$.
3. **Euler’s Theorem (2nd Degree):** Verify $x u_x + y u_y = 2u$ and $x^2 u_{xx} + 2xy u_{xy} + y^2 u_{yy} = 2u$ for $u(x, y) = \frac{4x^4 + 5y^4}{x^2 + y^2}$.
4. **3-Variable Jacobian:** For $x = 4r\sin\theta\cos\phi, \; y = 5r\sin\theta\sin\phi, \; z = 20r\cos\theta$, compute $J = \frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(r,\theta,\phi)}$ and verify $J \cdot J' = 1$.
